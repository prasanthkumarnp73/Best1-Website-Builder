WHY WE’RE HERE

Less busywork.
More room to grow.

Small shops have big ambitions, but too often their days are taken up by manual tasks and disconnected tools. We started Best1 Technologies to make helpful technology more accessible.

From a welcoming website to simpler billing, booking, and shop management, we help business owners spend less time juggling work and more time serving their customers. Our approach is personal, practical, and mindful of what a small business can invest.
